Step 1

Run the installer

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning — that's normal for new installers. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Step 2

Open it in Premiere Pro

Start Premiere Pro, then go to Window → Extensions → CapBeat. On first start the plugin downloads its AI model once — after that everything runs offline.

Bought Pro?

Activate your license

Your license key arrives by email right after checkout. Paste it into the License card in the CapBeat panel and click Activate — music mode and the Pro styles unlock instantly.

Heads up — you appear to be on a Mac. This installer is Windows-only and will not open on macOS. A Mac build is being worked on; email us and we'll tell you the day it ships.
