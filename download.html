SmartScreen flags every new installer it hasn't seen often enough yet — that's about CapBeat being new, not unsafe. The file comes straight from our GitHub release. Here's exactly what you'll see:

The warning disappears on its own once enough people have installed a version. Nothing is sent anywhere — CapBeat runs fully on your machine.

Step 1

Run the installer

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning — that's normal for new installers. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Step 2

Open it in Premiere Pro

Start Premiere Pro, then go to Window → Extensions → CapBeat. On first start the plugin downloads its AI model once — after that everything runs offline.

Bought Pro?

Activate your license

Your license key (CAPBEAT-…) arrives by email from Polar right after checkout — it's also shown on the order confirmation and at polar.sh/purchases. Paste it into the License card in the CapBeat panel and click Activate — music mode, all styles and all animations unlock instantly.

Heads up — you appear to be on a Mac. This installer is Windows-only and will not open on macOS. A Mac build is being worked on; email us and we'll tell you the day it ships.
