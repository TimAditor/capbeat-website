While you're here

Three things that came directly out of answers like yours.

From a user email

Full songs, start to finish

Music mode used to stop halfway through a track. Someone wrote in about it, and it turned out speech detection was silently dropping sung passages.

From a user email

Captions that stay put

Rendered captions used to land in a temporary folder that Windows cleans out. They now sit in a folder next to your project file.

From a user email

No Media Encoder needed

A tester had no Media Encoder installed and everything stalled. CapBeat now exports audio entirely on its own.
