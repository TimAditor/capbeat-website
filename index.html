Questions

Does it run on Mac?

Not yet. CapBeat needs Windows 10/11 and Premiere Pro 2023 or newer. Join the Mac waitlist.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your computer. The only download is the speech model, once.

Why does Windows warn me during installation?

The installer is signed by Morenotless. SmartScreen can still warn on a fresh version until it has enough downloads. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Step by step.

How do I activate Pro?

You get a license key by email. Paste it into the License tab of the panel. Pro covers 3 computers.
