Why everyone else fails on music

Speech recognition is trained on spoken language. Put a beat under the voice and it hears drums instead of consonants — and starts guessing. That’s why every guide repeats the same advice: for song lyrics, type them in by hand.

CapBeat adds a step no other Premiere Pro plugin has: a second neural network (Demucs, open-sourced by Meta) splits the mix into vocals and instrumental — locally, on your CPU. Recognition only ever hears the voice. Guessing becomes reading.

Web services exist that can do this — in exchange for uploads, subscriptions and a queue. On the Premiere timeline, with nothing leaving your machine, this plugin stands alone.

Honest answers to real questions

Why does Windows warn me during installation?

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning for any new installer that isn’t widely downloaded yet. Click “More info” → “Run anyway”. A signed build is in the works — the certificate is on order.

Does it run on Mac?

Not yet. Currently Windows 10/11 with Premiere Pro 2023 or newer. A Mac version is on the list, but we won’t name a date we can’t keep.

How good is music mode really?

On our reference clip: 14 recognized words without separation, 47 with — the complete lyric. It shines on clearly sung pop, rap and singer-songwriter material. Extreme metal vocals or very dense mixes remain hard even after separation. So: test your material with the free version first, then buy.

What happens after the 2 years of updates?

Your version keeps running unchanged, forever. You lose nothing — you just stop receiving new features until you decide, voluntarily, to upgrade to a future major version (discounted for existing customers).

Do I get my money back?

14 days, no questions asked, by email. We don’t want customers who feel burned.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. There is no server they could be uploaded to. The only internet connection is the one-time download of the AI models on first start (~1.5 GB speech, ~80 MB separation).

Premiere has built-in captions — why this?

Premiere’s transcription is fine for spoken language and plain subtitles. It can’t do word-by-word social-style animations, and it fails on music like everything else. CapBeat fills exactly those two gaps.
