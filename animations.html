Real motion blur, not a fake

Position keyframes alone can't blur in Premiere. CapBeat uses the same trick editors do by hand: it puts the movement on Premiere's own Transform effect and opens its shutter angle — automatically. One checkbox, and your fly-ins look like they were filmed, not slid.

Animations share the panel with word-by-word auto captions and the music mode that reads lyrics from a finished mix. One plugin, three jobs.
